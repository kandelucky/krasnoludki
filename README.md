# Krasnoludki

A trainer for the seven Polish noun cases (przypadki rzeczownika), for children, in Polish. Each case is a gnome
with its own hat colour, its questions and its helper word: *Celownik — Komu? Czemu? (się przyglądam)*. The order is
one sentence, shown on the home screen and in every drill: *Mam domek cały biały nad morzem wspaniałym*

- **Wszystkie razem**
  - **Tabela**: the seven cases with their questions, helper words and examples (*babcia, koszyki*)
  - **Kolejność**: the seven names mixed up; tap them from 1 to 7
  - **Pytania i nazwy**: a case's name → pick its questions, or the questions → pick the case
- **Po kolei**: one chapter per case (coming next)
- **Egzamin**: 15 cards, no help

A drill counts as memorized at 8 of 10 cards right and each under 5 seconds (Kolejność: all 7; the exam: 12 of 15).

To run it, open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step, and it works offline.
