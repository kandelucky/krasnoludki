# Krasnoludki

A trainer for the seven Polish noun cases (przypadki rzeczownika), for children, in Polish. Each case is a gnome
with its own hat colour, its questions and its helper word: *Celownik — Komu? Czemu? (się przyglądam)*.

- **Lekcja**: the seven gnomes one by one, then the order as one sentence: *Mama drozda celowo była na miejscu wypadku*
- **Pytania**: a case's name appears; pick its questions
- **Nazwy**: the questions appear; pick the case
- **Kolejność**: a case's name appears; pick its number, 1–7
- **Egzamin**: 15 cards from all three, no help

A drill counts as memorized at 8 of 10 cards right and each under 5 seconds (the exam: 12 of 15).

To run it, open `index.html` in a browser. There is no build step, and it works offline.
